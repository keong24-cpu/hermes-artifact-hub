The painful reality

The customer does not wake up wanting CRM. The boss wakes up asking: “Where did the sales go?”

Leak 01

Leads disappear in WhatsApp

Enquiries live inside personal chats. Management sees them only when someone chooses to report.

Leak 02

Quotes are sent, then forgotten

No clear next action, no aging alert, no quote-level follow-up discipline.

Leak 03

Discount and margin become invisible

Sales can look active while silently destroying gross profit.

The real enemy is not lack of software. It is management blindness before month-end results expose the damage.
